# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Okta Virtuoso does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with its developers or any developer-operated service. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

## How the extension handles data

Okta Virtuoso runs in your browser on supported Okta pages. When you use its features, it may read and process information available in your signed-in Okta session and make requests directly to that same Okta tenant. This information is used only to provide the feature you requested.

Data processed by the extension is not sent to the developers. CSV exports are generated locally and saved through your browser. Extension preferences, such as export-column selections and interface settings, are stored locally in your browser.

## Optional third-party service

The optional **Restore with Backupta** feature opens a Backupta URL that you configure. If you use it, your Okta tenant identifier and the identifiers of the objects you selected are included in that URL and are handled by the configured service under its own privacy practices. Okta Virtuoso does not operate or control that service.

## Permissions

Okta Virtuoso runs only on the supported Okta domains listed in its manifest. It uses those permissions to add its interface and provide Okta administration tools. It does not use those permissions to track browsing activity.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published in this repository with an updated effective date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy may be submitted through the repository's issue tracker.
